## Overview

A Mirror of My Becoming is a sovereign AI project — a body of work built locally on an M1 MacBook Air with 8GB of RAM. No cloud dependency. No external GPUs. No enterprise hardware.

The project consists of five working RAG pipelines ("ships"), each one testing a different architectural pattern — from standard retrieval-augmented generation to agentic reasoning to evidence-verified, fail-closed systems.

---

## The Approach

The project applies an auditor's lens to everything:

- **Verify** — do not trust
- **Document** — date everything, name everything, record the reasoning
- **Red team** — test the system adversarially
- **Fail closed** — when a claim cannot be verified, do not make it

---

## The Vision

Use AI to audit the cloud. Build systems that prove their own work.

Sovereign AI is not about avoiding the cloud — it's about owning the systems that matter.
